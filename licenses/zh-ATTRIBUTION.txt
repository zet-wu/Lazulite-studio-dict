Lazulite Studio fixed-reading dictionary packages

The converter and data have separate licenses. This repository's AGPL-3.0
license does not replace the licenses of the generated data.

- mozillazg/pinyin-data and mozillazg/phrase-pinyin-data: MIT.
  The selected merged files include material attributed by upstream to
  Unicode/Unihan, CC-CEDICT, zdic.net, zisea.com, guoxuedashi.com,
  Chinese character/phrase dictionaries and earlier pinyin projects.
  The complete upstream READMEs and their attribution links are included.
- Unicode/Unihan: Unicode License; Unicode, Inc.
- CC-CEDICT: CC-CEDICT contributors, https://cc-cedict.org/ and
  https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict .

  the current project uses CC-BY-SA-4.0. Both notices are retained for the
  aggregated upstream material. The generated adaptation is shared under
  CC-BY-SA-4.0, with the other original notices preserved.
- jieba: fxsjy and contributors, MIT; used only for word-frequency scores.
- OpenCC conversion data and Python implementation: Apache-2.0.

Changes made by this converter:
Structured segments and fixed reading units, deterministic deduplication,
fixed regional pronunciation conversion, optional orthographic query aliases
and automatically derived word costs. No hand-maintained word corrections.
Exact source commits and selected files are recorded in sources.json.

Converter source and reproducible build instructions:
https://github.com/zet-wu/Lazulite-studio-dict
