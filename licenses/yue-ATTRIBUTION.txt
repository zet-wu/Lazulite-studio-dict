Lazulite Studio fixed-reading dictionary packages

The converter and data have separate licenses. This repository's AGPL-3.0
license does not replace the licenses of the generated data.

- rime-cantonese / CanCLID and contributors: CC-BY-4.0.
  Only chars, words and essay-cantonese are selected; maps is not included.
- OpenCC conversion data and Python implementation: Apache-2.0.

Changes made by this converter:
Structured segments and fixed reading units, deterministic deduplication,
fixed regional pronunciation conversion, optional orthographic query aliases
and automatically derived word costs. No hand-maintained word corrections.
Exact source commits and selected files are recorded in sources.json.

Converter source and reproducible build instructions:
https://github.com/zet-wu/Lazulite-studio-dict
